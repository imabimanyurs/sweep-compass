Conditional figures apply after the first London-level break within the study’s NY window. They are not probabilities for a separate pre-08:00 sweep or a fresh 09:30 start. HTF trend and current location were not filters in the published sample.

The page’s displayed aggregates and conditional rates do not fully reconcile. Treat them as source-reported observations, not a calibrated forecasting model. Raw records and conditional sample counts are unavailable here. No live market feed.

Session clock & interpretation

All boundaries use New York time: Asia 20:00–02:00, London 02:00–08:00, NY 08:00–16:00. During US daylight time, IST equivalents are 05:30–11:30, 11:30–17:30, and 17:30–01:30 (+1 day). During US standard time, add one hour to those IST times.

“Open” means not yet broken since the London range was finalized. This tool does not measure order-block mitigation. Bullish or bearish overlays describe alignment or conflict, never a proven Judas reversal. Educational research; historical frequencies are not guaranteed outcomes.
